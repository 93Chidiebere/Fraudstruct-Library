## THEME 1: Graph Representation Learning & Foundational GNNs

1. **Semi-Supervised Classification with Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN)**  
   *Kipf & Welling (2017)* — Landmark spectral-GCN paper.  
   *Link:* [https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.02907](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.02907)

2. **Inductive Representation Learning on Large Graphs (GraphSAGE)**  
   *Hamilton et al. (2017)* — Introduces spatial neighborhood aggregation and sampling.  
   *Link:* [https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.02216](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.02216)

3. **Graph Attention Networks (GAT)**  
   *Veličković et al. (2018)* — Dynamic self-attention mechanisms on graph neighborhoods.  
   *Link:* [https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10903](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10903)

4. **How Powerful are Graph Neural Networks? (GIN)**  
   *Xu et al. (2018)* — Proves 1-WL isomorphism bound and limits of neighbor aggregation.  
   *Link:* [https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.00826](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.00826)

5. **Simplifying Graph Convolution (SGC)**  
   *Wu et al. (2019)* — ICML 2019 landmark paper proving linear graph smoothing collapses convolutions.  
   *Link:* [https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.07153](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.07153)

6. **BRIGHT: Graph Neural Networks in Real-Time Fraud Detection**  
   *Lu et al. (2022)* — ACM CIKM '22 paper introducing decoupled entity embeddings.  
   *Link:* [https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.13084](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.13084)

7. **GraphSAINT: Graph Sampling Based Inductive Engine**  
   *Zeng et al. (2020)* — ICLR 2020 subgraph sampling algorithm to scale GNN training.  
   *Link:* [https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.04931](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.04931)

8. **Cluster-GCN: An Efficient Algorithm for Training Deep GNNs**  
   *Chiang et al. (2019)* — ACM KDD 2019 paper partitioning graphs for fast batch training.  
   *Link:* [https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.07953](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.07953)

9. **Anti-Money Laundering in Bitcoin (Elliptic Dataset)**  
   *Weber et al. (2019)* — ACM KDD 2019 baseline GCN benchmarking on transaction graphs.  
   *Link:* [https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02591](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02591)
